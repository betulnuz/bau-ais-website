The Quiet Convergence: Why Every Frontier Model Started Looking the Same

By 2025, the architectural differences between the major frontier models had collapsed. This piece asks why — and what it means for the field.

Something quietly happened in 2025: the frontier models stopped being architecturally interesting. Not in terms of capability — those kept climbing — but in terms of design decisions. The bets converged. Everyone chose the same transformer variants, the same RLHF pipelines, the same inference-time scaling approaches.

Whether this is consolidation around proven ideas or premature closure on a design space that still has unexplored corners is the question this piece tries to answer honestly.

Deniz ArslanResearch Lead, BAU AIS
