2024

AI Revolution 360

21–22 December 2024 · BAU South Campus, Fazıl Say Concert Hall

Our first summit brought together professionals, academics, and students to discuss the impact of artificial intelligence across sectors, with speakers from NVIDIA, Microsoft, Amazon, IBM, TÜBİSAD, and Turkey's AI and startup ecosystem. Sessions covered digital transformation, data privacy and deepfakes, quantum computing, IoT, and AI in business.
